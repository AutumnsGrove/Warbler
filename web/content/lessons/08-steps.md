## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

Holding one note is half the skill. The other half is moving between notes on purpose. In this lesson you climb a staircase of five targets and come back down, one step at a time.

**How the staircase works.** Warbler draws five target lines: your base note, then four steps away from it. Each step is two semitones. You choose which way the stairs go:

- **Up and back:** base, up, up more, up, base.
- **Down and back:** base, down, down more, down, base.

Hold each step for 2 seconds. You can glide between steps or jump. Both count.

Each step shows its note name as you reach it. Watching the names change is a good way to start learning them.

## Practice

1. **Hear the steps.** Warbler plays the five notes in order. Hum along with them on "mm". (60s)
2. **Walk the stairs, slowly.** Hum all five steps on your own, holding each for 2 seconds. Do it once. (45s)
3. **Same on "ah".** Do the staircase on "ah", 3 times. (120s)
4. **Smaller steps.** Warbler shrinks each step to one semitone. Do one full staircase on "ah". (45s)
5. **Bigger steps.** Warbler widens each step to three semitones. Do one full staircase on "ah". (45s)

## Done

- A picture of your path overlaid on the staircase.
- The note name you landed on for each step.
- Feel tags (optional).
