## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

Holding a pitch on a hum is easy. Doing it while saying words is harder. This lesson builds the bridge in three stages, repeated for each phrase.

1. **Hum.** Match your base note on "mm". Hold it for 1 second.
2. **Chant.** Say the phrase on that one note, like a flat singsong.
3. **Speak.** Say the phrase at normal pace and rhythm, with the target line hidden.

The screen always shows which stage you are on ("Stage 2 of 3: chant"), and moves on by itself when you complete one.

In stage 3 your pitch will wander away from the note. That is normal. Warbler shows you the average pitch of your sentence so you can see how close it stayed.

The phrases use smooth, voiced sounds like "m", "n", "l", "w" and vowels. These keep your voice on the whole time, so the graph stays clear.

## Practice

Do each phrase through all three stages.

1. **"Many men move more."** Hum, chant, speak. (90s)
2. **"We were all away."** Hum, chant, speak. (90s)
3. **"Lemon jam on my lamb."** Hum, chant, speak. (90s)
4. **Pick one to repeat.** Choose the phrase you liked best and go through the three stages again. (90s)

## Done

- For each phrase: the average pitch of your stage 3 speech, and its distance from the target in semitones.
- Feel tags (optional).
