## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

Holding "ee" on its own is easy. Keeping your chosen sound across real words is harder, because the tongue and lips move constantly.

This lesson uses the chart to explore. You will say the same words twice: once as you normally do, and once while moving your vowels toward a position you choose (more forward or more back, brighter or darker). The chart shows how far each version moved.

Neither position is the correct one. If you have a resonance zone set in Settings, it appears as an outline, and you can aim for it. If not, just explore.

## Practice

1. **Word list, natural.** Say these words on your base note, one per breath: "key, bay, tie, meet, seen, lean". Watch the cloud. (60s)
2. **Word list, moved.** Say the same words again, shifting your tongue and lips toward the position you want. Watch the cloud move. (60s)
3. **Compare.** Warbler overlays the two clouds and shows how far apart their middles are, as a percentage. (30s)
4. **Short phrases.** Say "We see the green sea" and "Mean Eve needs tea," once natural and once moved. (90s)
5. **A sentence.** Say "Every evening, we eat green beans and cheese" at normal speed, first natural, then moved. (90s)
6. **See the spread.** Warbler shows the middle of each cloud and how much the position wandered during the sentence. (30s)

## Done

- The two clouds from each round, with approximate F1 and F2 for the middle of each.
- Feel tags (optional).
