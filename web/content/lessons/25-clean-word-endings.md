## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

In everyday speech the last sound of a word often gets shortened or dropped, especially when you are going fast. Finishing the last sound lightly makes words easier to follow.

The evidence for this as a voice skill is thin. This is a short optional lesson built on the same general idea as the last one: give your tongue and lips enough time to finish each sound.

Light is the key. A heavy, popped ending sounds forced and strains the voice. You are aiming for a clean, quick finish.

## Practice

1. **Single words.** Say these slowly, finishing the last sound lightly: "cat, bed, bus, back, hand, help". (45s)
2. **Pairs.** Say "bat, bad", "back, bag", "bus, buzz". Make the final sounds different. Replay and check. (60s)
3. **Short phrase.** Say "Last night's late snack" and "Hot soup and fresh bread" at a steady pace. (45s)
4. **Sentence.** Say "We kept the lamp on until midnight" once at your usual pace, once giving the endings a clean finish. Replay both. (60s)
