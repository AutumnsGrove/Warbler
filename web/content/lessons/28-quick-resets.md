## Warm-up

- (30s) Sit upright, sip some water, and breathe out slowly twice.

## Learn

During a normal day your voice drifts. You get tired, rushed or distracted, and your speech goes back to its usual patterns. That is normal.

A quick reset is a 60-second routine to get back on track. An NHS leaflet says the straw exercises can also be used to reset when tension builds. Here you build a routine that works without a straw, so you can do it anywhere.

After this lesson, the Today screen has a "Quick reset" button that guides you through it.

## Practice

Build your routine, then run it.

1. **Breath.** Breathe in for 3 and out for 4, three times. (20s)
2. **Release.** Drop your jaw and shoulders. Blow gentle lip bubbles, or hum softly. (15s)
3. **Find the note.** Hum your base note. Hold it in the band for 2 seconds. (10s)
4. **Say your anchor phrase.** Pick one of your anchor phrases and say it once at your usual pace. (10s)
5. **Run it together.** Do steps 1 to 4 straight through, in 60 seconds. (60s)
6. **Do it again.** Run it a second time, a little faster. (45s)

## Done

- Your routine is saved on the Today screen as "Quick reset".
