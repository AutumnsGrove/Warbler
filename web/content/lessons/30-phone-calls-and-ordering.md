## Warm-up

- (60s) Relaxed breathing: in for 3, out for 4, five times. Then hum your base note for 5 seconds.

## Learn

A phone call takes away everything except your voice. That makes short calls good practice: the other person can only go by what they hear.

Warbler's practice calls are pre-recorded. The caller says the next line when you finish speaking, so it feels like a real exchange. You can hang up at any time, and there is no score.

A card on screen tells you who you are calling and what you might say. You do not have to follow it word for word.

## Practice

1. **Read the card.** Check who you are calling and the two or three lines you might use. (30s)
2. **Rehearse.** Say your opening line out loud twice with the band showing. (30s)
3. **Make the call.** Start "Ordering a pizza". The phone rings and the caller picks up. Have your conversation. (180s)
4. **Hang up and look.** The summary shows your pitch and pauses for the call. Replay it with both voices. (60s)
5. **Choose.** Make a second call with a different scenario, or finish. (15s)

## Done

- The call is saved in History as a practice call, and the replay plays both sides.
