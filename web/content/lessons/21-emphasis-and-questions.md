## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

Where you put the emphasis changes what a sentence means. Say "I didn't say she took it" and stress a different word each time, and you get a different meaning each time.

Emphasis usually shows up as a pitch peak on the stressed word. A question usually shows up as a rise at the end. In this lesson you place the peaks and the rises exactly where you choose.

The aim is control, not copying a model. Warbler marks where your pitch peaked so you can see whether it landed on the word you meant.

## Practice

1. **Seven meanings.** Say "I didn't say she took it" seven times. Each time, lift your pitch on the next word: "I", "didn't", "say", "she", "took", "it". Watch where the peak lands. (120s)
2. **Statement and question.** Say "You're coming" falling at the end, as a statement. Then say it rising at the end, as a question. Repeat 4 times. (60s)
3. **Longer questions.** Say "Are you coming tomorrow?" with a rise on the last word. Then say "What time are you coming?" ending low. Do each 3 times. (60s)
4. **Stress pairs.** Say "REcord" (noun) and "reCORD" (verb), "PREsent" and "preSENT". Watch the peak move to the stressed part. (60s)
5. **Your pick.** Say any short sentence three times, each time stressing a different word. Warbler marks each peak. (60s)

## Done

- A marker on each version showing where your pitch peaked.
- Feel tags (optional).
