## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

Anchor phrases are short things you say every day without thinking: "hi", "thanks", your name, your usual coffee order. If you practise how you say them, you get many chances a day to use what you have been training.

You choose the phrases. Warbler saves them in Settings so you can practise them any time.

## Practice

1. **Choose five.** Pick five phrases from the list or type your own. The list has: "Hi, how are you?", "Thanks so much.", "Could I get a coffee, please?", "My name is...", "See you later." (60s)
2. **Each one, three times.** Say each phrase three times with your target band showing. (90s)
3. **Chain them.** Say all five in order, as if in a quick conversation, with a breath between each. (45s)
4. **Save your best.** Warbler marks the take with the smoothest pitch. Replay it and keep it as your reference clip. (45s)
5. **Pick one for today.** Choose one phrase to use at least once in real life today. (15s)

## Done

- The five phrases are saved in Settings.
- Your reference clip is saved in History.
