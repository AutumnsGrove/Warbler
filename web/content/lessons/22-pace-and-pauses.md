## Warm-up

- (60s) Relaxed breathing: in for 3, out for 4, five times, one hand on your belly.

## Learn

Speed and pauses shape how speech comes across. A short pause lets you take a breath, gives the listener time to follow, and gives you time to choose your next pitch.

A standard clear-speech strategy in NHS speech therapy material is to slow down, pause between phrases, and breathe before you speak. Here you practise it with numbers: Warbler shows how long you took, how many pauses you made, and how long they were.

There is no best speed. This lesson helps you control it.

## Practice

Read these three sentences each time:

"On Tuesday, I took the long way home along the old railway path. The evening was mild, and the trees were turning gold. I stopped by the bridge to watch the water move under it."

1. **Fast.** Read it as fast as you comfortably can. Warbler shows your time and your pauses. (30s)
2. **Usual.** Read it at your usual pace. (30s)
3. **Slower.** Read it a little slower than usual, with a breath before each sentence. (45s)
4. **Planned pauses.** Read it with a clear pause at each comma and full stop, about half a second each. Warbler counts them. (60s)
5. **Off script.** Talk for 30 seconds about your morning. Pause between ideas. The pause counter updates as you go. (45s)

## Done

- Time, number of pauses, and average pause length for each version.
- Feel tags (optional).
