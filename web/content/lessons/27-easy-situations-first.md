## Warm-up

- (60s) Relaxed breathing: in for 3, out for 4, five times, one hand on your belly.

## Learn

Using your voice in real life is easier if you start with the situations that feel easiest and work up. One voice training study had people rank everyday speaking situations from easiest to hardest and used that order to plan practice.

You do the ranking, and nobody else sees it. You decide whether and when to try any of them. Choose situations where you feel safe and comfortable.

## Practice

1. **Sort the cards.** Warbler shows 15 everyday situations. Drag them into order from easiest to hardest for you. Remove any you do not want. (150s)
2. **Look at the top three.** These are your starting situations. (15s)
3. **Rehearse the first one.** Say one or two sentences you would use in your easiest situation, out loud, with your target band showing. (60s)
4. **Plan a tiny try.** Choose one small real-life moment from your top three. You can skip this step. (30s)

Situations on the cards:

- Saying hello to a neighbour
- Talking to a friend
- Ordering a drink at a counter
- Asking a shop assistant where something is
- Ordering food at a restaurant
- Answering the phone
- Calling a business
- Talking to a family member
- Introducing yourself to someone new
- Speaking in a small group
- Leaving a voicemail
- Talking to a colleague
- Giving your details to a receptionist
- Making a booking by phone
- Chatting while waiting in a queue

## Done

- Your ranking is saved in Settings. You can redo it any time.
