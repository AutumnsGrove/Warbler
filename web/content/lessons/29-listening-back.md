## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

You hear your own voice differently while you speak, partly through the bones of your head. A recording is closer to what other people hear.

In this lesson you listen back to one of your own sessions, find one thing you like and one thing to work on, then try it again straight away.

## Practice

1. **Pick a session.** Warbler suggests your most recent one. You can pick another from History. (20s)
2. **Listen.** Play a 30-second stretch while watching the pitch graph. (45s)
3. **Jump to the extremes.** Warbler marks the highest and lowest points in that stretch. Play the second before and after each. (60s)
4. **Choose.** Pick one moment you liked and one thing you would change. (30s)
5. **Try it again.** Say the same sentence from the session now, changing only that one thing. (60s)
6. **Compare.** Replay the original and the new version back to back. (45s)

## Done

- The new take is saved in History next to the original.
