## Warm-up

About 1 minute.

- (30s) Sit comfortably and take a sip of water.
- (30s) Three slow breaths, letting your shoulders drop each time you breathe out.

## Learn

About 3 minutes.

Most of what keeps a voice comfortable happens outside practice time. The advice below comes from the US National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders (NIDCD) and an NHS hospital leaflet. It is general voice-health advice from health organisations. It is not the result of experiments, and none of it is specific to any one voice goal.

**Water and moisture**

- Drink plenty of water. The NHS leaflet says about 2 litres of fluid a day is about right.
- If you have caffeine or alcohol, balance it with plenty of water. The NHS leaflet notes that alcohol can leave the vocal folds dry and inflamed.
- NIDCD suggests a humidifier at home, especially in winter or dry climates, and mentions 30 percent humidity.

**Things that irritate**

- Smoke, including other people's smoke, irritates the voice folds.
- Shouting, screaming and talking over noise all put stress on the voice. Try not to talk in very noisy places.
- Both talking too loudly and too softly can stress your voice.

**Throat clearing**

Clearing your throat again and again is hard on the voice. The NHS leaflet suggests these instead: sip water, swallow hard, yawn, or suck a sugar-free sweet.

**Body habits**

- Do not hold your phone between your head and shoulder for long calls (NIDCD).
- Sit in an open, relaxed position (NHS leaflet).
- NIDCD notes that being tired hurts the voice, and that regular exercise helps stamina, posture and breathing. The NHS leaflet mentions 6 to 9 hours of sleep.

**Stomach acid**

NIDCD says spicy foods can trigger acid coming up into the throat. If you often have heartburn, it recommends talking to your doctor.

## Practice

About 4 minutes. **Stop if you feel pain, strain, tightness or hoarseness. Rest and drink some water.**

No microphone is used in this lesson.

1. **Pick three.** Choose three small things from the lists above that you would like to try this week. For example: keep a water bottle nearby, swap throat clearing for a sip of water, or hold your phone in your hand instead of against your shoulder. Say them to yourself or write them on a sticky note. (90s)
2. **Practise the swap.** Imagine the urge to clear your throat. Instead: take a sip of water, swallow hard, or yawn. Try each once so you know which feels best. (60s)
3. **Check your sitting.** Notice how you are sitting right now. Uncross your legs and arms if they are crossed. Sit tall without stiffening. (30s)
4. **Pick a quiet moment.** Think of one time today when you could give your voice a short quiet break. (30s)
