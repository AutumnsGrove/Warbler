## Warm-up

- (30s) Sit upright, sip some water, and breathe out slowly twice.

## Learn

Most voice care happens outside practice time. This is general advice from the US National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders (NIDCD) and an NHS hospital leaflet. It comes from health organisations, not from experiments.

- **Water.** Drink plenty. The NHS leaflet says about 2 litres of fluid a day is about right.
- **Caffeine and alcohol.** Balance them with extra water. The NHS leaflet notes alcohol can leave the voice folds dry and inflamed.
- **Dry air.** NIDCD suggests a humidifier at home, especially in winter, and mentions 30 percent humidity.
- **Smoke.** It irritates the voice folds, including other people's.
- **Noise.** Talking over loud noise, shouting and screaming all stress the voice.
- **Throat clearing.** It is hard on the voice. The NHS leaflet suggests a sip of water, a hard swallow, a yawn, or a sugar-free sweet instead.
- **Phone.** Do not hold it between your head and shoulder for long calls (NIDCD).
- **Heartburn.** NIDCD says spicy food can bring acid up into the throat. If heartburn is frequent, it recommends talking to a doctor.

## Practice

No microphone this time. Do these now.

1. **Water.** Fill a glass or bottle and put it where you will see it. (30s)
2. **Swap drill.** Pretend you need to clear your throat. Do each swap once: sip, hard swallow, yawn. Keep the one you like best. (60s)
3. **Phone.** Hold your phone in your hand with your head level. Hold it that way for the rest of this lesson. (30s)
4. **Pick one.** Choose one more habit from the list to try today. (30s)
