## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

Your lips change the length of the space in front of your tongue. Round and push them forward and the space gets longer, which lowers the resonances of the sound. Spread them gently, like a relaxed smile, and the space gets shorter, which raises them. Voice training studies have included lip spreading among the techniques used.

In plain terms: rounded lips sound darker, spread lips sound brighter, and everything in between is a dial you can turn.

Keep it gentle. A strained, tight smile makes tension, not resonance.

## Practice

Keep the pitch line flat on your base note throughout.

1. **Three lip shapes on "ah".** Hold "ah" with lips relaxed for 3 seconds. Then round them forward as if for "oo", keeping the "ah" sound. Then spread them gently. Do the cycle 3 times. (90s)
2. **Replay.** Listen back. Can you hear the difference between all three? (30s)
3. **Word pairs.** Say each pair on your base note: "see, sue", "me, moo", "bee, boo". Notice how the lip shape changes between the two words. (60s)
4. **A sentence, two ways.** Say "We see the green trees" once with relaxed lips, and once with lips slightly spread throughout. Replay both. (90s)
5. **Dial it.** Say the sentence once more, choosing the lip shape you like best today. (30s)
