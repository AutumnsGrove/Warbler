## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

The chart shows two resonances at once. F1 (how open your jaw is) runs up the side. F2 (how far forward your tongue is) runs along the bottom. Every vowel you say lands as a dot.

Each dot is a cloud of your recent sounds, and the larger dot is the middle of the cloud. The numbers are estimates, and when Warbler is not confident about a sound, it shows nothing.

Formant numbers differ from person to person, partly because of vocal tract length. Your own chart is the one that matters, and you compare it with your own earlier charts.

If the dots look scattered or odd, check Settings, where the formant ceiling can be adjusted. A hint there explains what it does.

## Practice

1. **Find the corners.** Hold your base note and say "ee", "ah", "oo", 3 seconds each. Watch three clusters appear. Repeat 3 times. (90s)
2. **Slide across.** Glide from "oo" to "ee" and watch the dot move along the F2 axis. (45s)
3. **Slide down.** Glide from "ee" to "ah" and watch it move along the F1 axis. (45s)
4. **Make one dot move.** Say "ah" and move your tongue forward and back. The dot should shift sideways. (60s)
5. **Your zones.** If you have set a resonance zone in Settings, it appears as a soft outline. Say a sentence and see where the cloud lands. (60s)
