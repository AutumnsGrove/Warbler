## Warm-up

- (60s) Jaw check from the tension lesson: let your jaw hang loose, lips closed. Then straw or lip bubbles, 5 times.

## Learn

Clear speech comes from giving your jaw, lips and tongue enough time and room to move. When speech is rushed, those moves get small and blurred.

The evidence for articulation practice in voice training is thin, and this lesson is based on general clear-speech strategies from NHS speech therapy material: slow down a little, leave room to move, and break long sentences into parts.

Warbler cannot measure clarity. Your ears, through replay, are the check.

Keep the movement easy. Big does not mean tense.

## Practice

1. **Jaw.** Say "ma ma ma ma" slowly, letting your jaw drop easily on each "ma". Ten times. (30s)
2. **Lips.** Alternate "oo" and "ee" slowly, rounding then spreading your lips. Ten times. (30s)
3. **Tongue.** Say "la la la la" with the tip of your tongue tapping lightly behind your top teeth. Ten times. (30s)
4. **Slow twister.** Say "red lorry, yellow lorry" slowly, five times, giving every sound room. Then at normal speed, five times. (60s)
5. **Two versions.** Say "Please bring three black bags to the Saturday market" in your usual way. Then say it again, giving each word a little more time. Replay both. (90s)
6. **Break it up.** Read "Every Saturday morning, I walk down to the market by the river" in two parts with a short pause in the middle. Replay. (60s)
