## Warm-up

- (30s) Sit comfortably, sip some water, and breathe out slowly twice.

## Learn

Rest is part of practice. NIDCD recommends "vocal naps": short breaks for your voice throughout the day. It also says not to speak or sing when your voice is hoarse or tired.

Whispering is not rest. An NHS leaflet warns that it can still cause damage. For a real break, stay quiet.

The straw exercise from the warm-up lesson also works as a cool-down or a reset when your voice feels tense, according to the NHS leaflet.

**Call it a day when:**

- your voice feels weaker than when you started
- the same sound takes more effort
- anything hurts, strains or feels tight

Stopping early is always fine.

## Practice

1. **Cool-down.** Use the straw in water, or blow bubbles through loosely closed lips. Add a soft "oo" and let it slide down at the end. 5 times, light and easy. (60s)
2. **Sip.** Drink some water and swallow slowly. (15s)
3. **Voice nap.** Stay completely quiet for 60 seconds. No whispering, humming or throat clearing. The timer counts down. (60s)
4. **Plan a nap.** Pick one time in the next few hours when you can give your voice a 5-minute break. (30s)
