## Warm-up

About 1 minute.

- (30s) Sit comfortably and sip some water.
- (30s) Breathe slowly: in through your nose, out through your mouth.

## Learn

About 2 minutes.

**Resting is part of training.** NIDCD recommends taking "vocal naps", which means resting your voice at several points in the day. It also says to avoid speaking or singing when your voice is hoarse or tired, and notes that being physically tired has a negative effect on the voice.

**Whispering is not rest.** It feels gentle, but an NHS leaflet warns that whispering can still cause damage. NIDCD also lists whispering, along with screaming, as a vocal extreme to avoid. If your voice needs rest, real quiet is best.

**Cooling down.** The same straw exercise from the warm-up lesson can also be used afterward as an easy cool-down, or as a way to reset when your voice feels tense, according to the NHS leaflet.

**How to know when to stop for the day.** Take a break or end your practice if you notice:

- your voice feels tired or weaker than when you started
- you need more effort to make the same sound
- you feel pain, strain, tightness or hoarseness

You do not have to wait until all of these show up. Stopping early is always fine.

## Practice

About 5 minutes. **Stop if you feel pain, strain, tightness or hoarseness. Rest and drink some water.**

1. **Easy cool-down.** Use the straw in water, or blow gentle bubbles through loosely closed lips. Make a soft "oo" and let it slide down gently at the end, five times. Keep everything light and easy. (60s)
2. **Sip and swallow.** Take a slow sip of water. (15s)
3. **Voice nap.** Stay completely quiet for 60 seconds. No whispering, no humming, no throat clearing. The timer will count down. Notice how your throat feels. (60s)
4. **Plan your rest.** Think about the next hour. When will you speak a lot? When can you give your voice a short quiet break? You do not need to write anything down. (60s)

## Done

- Summary of the session.
- Feel tags (optional).
- Reminder: if your voice felt tight or scratchy today, take extra rest. That is a good decision.
