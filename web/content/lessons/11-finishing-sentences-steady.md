## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

In most speech, pitch drifts downward over the course of a sentence. Researchers call this declination, and it is one of the most widely studied features of speech. Your sentences probably do it too.

Neither a drifting ending nor a level ending is right or wrong. This lesson is about control: seeing what your sentences do, then choosing.

Warbler measures it for you. For each sentence it compares the pitch at the start with the pitch at the end and shows the difference in semitones. A negative number means the pitch fell. A positive number means it rose.

## Practice

Use these sentences, or your own:

- "Many of my neighbours were away in May."
- "I will make the lemon jam on Monday."
- "The yellow lamp was on all evening."

1. **Natural.** Say all three at your normal pace. Warbler shows the start-to-end change for each. (60s)
2. **Chanted level.** Chant each sentence on your base note, flat all the way through. (60s)
3. **Spoken level.** Say each sentence normally, but keep the end at the same pitch as the start. The band helps. (90s)
4. **Chosen fall.** Say each sentence and let the last word drop by about two semitones. Warbler shows how far you dropped. (60s)
5. **Compare.** Warbler overlays your natural, level and falling versions of one sentence. (30s)

## Done

- Start-to-end change in semitones for each version of each sentence.
- Feel tags (optional).
