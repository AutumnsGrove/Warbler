## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

This lesson puts the earlier prosody skills together: pitch movement, emphasis and pauses. You read a short text three times, changing how you use them.

The text is not the weekly passage. The weekly passage never changes, so that your weekly recordings stay comparable. Practice texts can be as varied as you like.

The three versions are all valid. This is about finding what you like.

## Practice

Text:

"Last spring, I planted a small garden behind my house. I started with beans, mint, and a few tomatoes. Every morning, I would water them before making coffee. Some days, a little brown bird visited the fence and sang for a while. Would it come back tomorrow? I never knew."

1. **Plain.** Read it in a flat, even voice. (45s)
2. **Moving.** Read it again, with big pitch movement. Pick three words to stress, and let the question rise at the end. (60s)
3. **Paced.** Read it again with planned pauses after each sentence. (60s)
4. **Blend.** Read it a fourth time, taking what you liked from the others. (60s)
5. **Compare.** Warbler shows the four versions side by side: pitch range, pauses and time. Replay the one you like best. (60s)

## Done

- The four versions saved in History so you can replay and compare.
- Pitch range, pause count and time for each.
