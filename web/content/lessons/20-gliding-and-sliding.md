## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

Intonation is the way your pitch moves across words and sentences. It is what makes speech sound lively or flat, calm or excited, like a statement or a question.

The aim of this lesson is control. You will practise moving your pitch smoothly, up and down, on purpose. There is no model to copy and no right shape. You decide later how much movement you want in everyday speech.

Warbler shows the pitch range you used in each version, in semitones.

## Practice

1. **Glide.** Hum "mm" and glide slowly up to a comfortable high note, then back down. Do it 3 times. (45s)
2. **One word, two shapes.** Say "yes" with a falling pitch, like a firm answer. Then say "yes" with a rising pitch, like a question. Do each 4 times. (60s)
3. **Longer words.** Say "really", "maybe" and "tomorrow", first with a rise, then with a fall. (60s)
4. **Move the peak.** Say "I'm going to the shop" four times. Each time, lift your pitch on a different word: first "I'm", then "going", then "shop". Watch where the peak lands. (90s)
5. **Small and big.** Read "The weather was lovely on Saturday" with a flat, narrow pitch. Then again with big movements. Then once in the middle. Warbler shows the range for each. (90s)
6. **Words that change.** Say these pairs, putting the stress on the capital letters: "REcord / reCORD", "PREsent / preSENT". Watch the pitch peak move. (45s)
