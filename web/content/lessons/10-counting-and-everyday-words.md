## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Slide up and down 5 times.

## Learn

Counting, days of the week and greetings are words you say without thinking. That makes them useful practice material: you can focus on where your pitch sits, because you do not have to think about what to say.

Your target band stays on the graph. Each round ends with a summary of your median pitch, which is the middle value of everything you said. Half of your speaking was above it and half below.

## Practice

1. **Count 1 to 10 on a chant.** Chant on the base note, like a flat singsong. (45s)
2. **Count 1 to 20, speaking.** Say the numbers in your normal voice with the band showing. (60s)
3. **Days and months.** Say the days of the week, then the months of the year, at a normal pace. (90s)
4. **Greetings.** Say five greetings, one per breath: "Hello." "Good morning." "How are you?" "Nice to meet you." "See you later." (90s)
5. **Read your results.** Warbler shows the median pitch for each set next to your band. (30s)

## Done

- Median pitch for each set, and its distance from your base note in semitones.
- Feel tags (optional).
