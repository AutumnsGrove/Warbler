## Warm-up

- (60s) Straw or lip bubbles with a soft "oo". Add a slide up and down, 5 times.

## Learn

Pitch control means putting your voice where you mean to. This lesson trains that with a simple game: a line on the graph is the target, and you match it.

**Your base note** is the note nearest to your usual speaking pitch from the last lesson. Warbler draws it as a flat line. You can pick a different note if you prefer.

**What "close" means.** Around the line is a soft band. The default band is one semitone either side of the line. At 200 Hz that is about 12 Hz. Stay inside the band for 1 second and the round is complete. You can narrow the band later in the lesson.

There is no fail state. If you drift out, keep going. The round completes when you hold it.

You can tap the play button at any time to hear the target note.

## Practice

1. **Hear it.** Tap play to hear the base note. Hum along with it. (30s)
2. **Match on "mm".** Hum the base note and hold it inside the band for 1 second. Warbler marks the round complete. Do 5 rounds. (90s)
3. **Match on "ah".** Same thing on an open "ah". 5 rounds. (90s)
4. **Narrow the band.** The band shrinks to half a semitone either side. 3 rounds on "ah". (60s)
5. **Move the target.** Warbler shifts the target by two semitones. You choose up or down. Match it for 3 rounds. (90s)

## Done

- Rounds completed, and your average distance from the target during the holds, in semitones.
- Feel tags (optional).
